# Analysis

This folder documents the analysis and interpretation of the experimental results from the SiGC composite anode study.

## Characterization Methods

The study used multiple techniques to evaluate the structure and electrochemical behavior of the composites:

- X-ray diffraction (XRD)
- Energy-dispersive spectroscopy (EDS)
- Elemental mapping
- Transmission electron microscopy (TEM)
- Cyclic voltammetry (CV)
- Galvanostatic charge-discharge testing
- Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS)

## Electrochemical Analysis

The electrochemical analysis focused on:

- Initial specific capacity
- Capacity retention during cycling
- Charge-discharge behavior
- Reproducibility across multiple half-cells
- Comparison between sieved and unsieved silicon

The reported results show that the unsieved composite achieved slightly higher initial capacity, while the sieved composite demonstrated better capacity retention after 100 cycles.
